VoiceDesk Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

VoiceDesk is an order manager for small shops. It is a personal project built and run by Omkar Warik. This page explains what data VoiceDesk collects, why, and how you can have it deleted.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in (with email or Google) through Clerk, we receive your name and email address. These are used only to create and identify your account.
  • Orders and customer details. Orders, customer names, phone numbers and notes that you add are stored in our database.
  • Chat messages. Your messages to the assistant and its replies are stored so your conversations can be restored when you come back.
  • Voice recordings. When you use voice input, the audio is sent to Groq to be converted to text. The audio is not stored; only the resulting transcript text is kept, as part of your chat.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run the app. Each user's data is visible only to their own account.

Services we use

  • Clerk for sign-in and authentication.
  • Groq for speech-to-text and the language model that powers the assistant.
  • Neon for the database.
  • DigitalOcean and Cloudflare for hosting.

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data deleted, email omkarwarik1204@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with.
